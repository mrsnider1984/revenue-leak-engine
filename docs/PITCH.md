# Live pitch

Six beats. Working software carries beat 5. Do not read the architecture.

## 1. Human story

"I just want to decompress."

One private hour for two, after a stressful week. Oasis Hot Tub Gardens, Ann Arbor. The person is not thinking about conversion. If the plan stops feeling real, they think "forget it."

## 2. Problem

That opportunity can stop moving while the owner still has no one explanation of where, or why.

The owner can see a website, a booking request, and the front desk. They cannot see one investigation of this customer.

## 3. Evidence

Two numbers, both narrower than this product.

Scale. Washtenaw County had 8,322 employer establishments in 2023. That is every establishment with paid employees, from the U.S. Census County Business Patterns, republished by Health for All Washtenaw. It is not a count of appointment businesses, and it is not Oasis.

https://www.healthforallwashtenaw.org/indicators/index/view?indicatorId=6371&localeId=1363

Consequence. Baymard, updated September 22, 2025: 17% of US online shoppers have abandoned an order because checkout was too long or complicated. That is ecommerce checkout. It is not Oasis, and it is not appointment booking.

https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate

No customer interview is claimed. No dollar loss is claimed.

## 4. Solution

Revenue Leak Engine follows a customer opportunity across a local business journey, identifies where progress may have stopped, challenges the suspected cause against evidence, and tells the owner what to test first.

## 5. Live demo

Mission → Investigate journey → Evidence → Skeptic → Rejected finding → Action ready → Fix first → Monitor.

Say the price finding was rejected. Say the revenue impact is unquantified. Say the handoff is synthetic.

## 6. Jac and IBM

The opportunity graph, the edges, and the verdicts are Jac. Walkers write the graph. The React screen reads it.

IBM Granite is implemented as the Skeptic client and is not called in this demo. No credentials are configured. The verdict on screen is labeled `deterministic_rules`.

## 7. Michigan, then the next step

The proof case is Oasis Hot Tub Gardens in Ann Arbor. The operational handoff is synthetic and labeled. It was not provided by Oasis.

A later pilot would replace that handoff with first-party confirmation counts, then monitor whether completion moved. Distribution, if the pilot is real, is through local economic-development groups, SBDCs, and chambers. That path is not built this weekend.
